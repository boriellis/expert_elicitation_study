Pacific Seabird Distribution Dataset

The species distribution models (SDMs) developed by Leirness et al. (2021) describe the seasonal distributions of seabirds in the POCS region. In the maps below, you will find the model predictions for each species aggregated across seasons (i.e., one annual map per species).

Important notes

  • Due to data limitations or high proportions of unidentified observations (e.g., “loon sp.”) for particular taxa, some of the original models were produced for species groups instead of individual species.

  • The maps below have been simplified to aid interpretation and comparison between species. The zone marked in light blue can be interpreted as the species range within the region. Within that zone, yellow represents the region that contains 95% of the predicted density for the species, and red represents the region that contains the core 50% of density. The zone demarcated in grey shows the overall study area.

  • Note that in some cases distributions are highly concentrated, making the visualization of distribution challenging - we recommend zooming in if a map looks blank at a glance.
